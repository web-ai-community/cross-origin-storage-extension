Cross-Origin Storage

Anonymous usage statistics

To help shape the Cross-Origin Storage API, the extension shares anonymous statistics about which widely used files sit in COS caches and how often sites reuse them. You can turn this off now or any time later in the extension's options.

In Firefox, this setting is the "technical and interaction data" permission, which you can also change in the add-on's permissions in about:addons.

Contents of a report

  • Once a week, for each file in your COS cache that is on the Public Hash List, a public list of widely deployed files: its hash, size, MIME type, who it is shared with (everyone, listed origins, or the same site), and how many origins and sites have used it. Other files are only counted, grouped by the general kind of file (such as font/*), with their total size rounded.
  • Once a week, totals for your COS cache, as shown in the extension's popup: the number of files, their total size, how many origins and sites have used it, and the space saved by sharing. Sizes are rounded.
  • Once a day, how often files on the Public Hash List were found (hits), not found (misses), or stored, with the bytes involved, through which part of the API, and for hits, whether the requesting site is the one that stored the file. Files not on the list are again only counted.
  • The extension version, the browser (Chrome, Firefox, or Safari), and a random ID that is replaced every week.

Reports never contain URLs, origins, site names, or the hash of any file outside the Public Hash List. Origins and sites are only counted. Pages served from localhost and .test domains aren't counted. Nothing is sent until a day after this page first opened. Reports go to a Google Apps Script that writes them to a spreadsheet; the script doesn't receive or store IP addresses. See the privacy policy for details.
